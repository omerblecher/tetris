Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Falling Blocks Classic ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed by Omer Blecher, handles your information when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum information needed to operate the app. This may include:

  • An anonymous identifier assigned by Firebase when you play as a guest
  • Your display name, email address, profile photo URL, and a unique user ID provided by Google — only if you choose to sign in with Google
  • Your best score in the game, stored in our database

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To display your name on the leaderboard
  • To save and retrieve your personal best score
  • To authenticate you across sessions

We do not use your information for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any other purpose.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). One document per user is stored, containing your display name and best score. Firebase uses industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest, and access is restricted by Firebase Security Rules. Firebase's privacy policy applies: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties. Your display name and score are visible to other players on the public leaderboard.

5. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services, each subject to their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Google Sign-In

6. Sign-In is Optional

You can play the game without signing in. Signing in is only required to submit your score to the leaderboard.

7. Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data, contact us at the email below. We will remove your account data from our database within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions, data access requests, or deletion requests? Contact us at: omerblecher@gmail.com
